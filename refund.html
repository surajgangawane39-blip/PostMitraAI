Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may request cancellation of your PostMitra AI Pro subscription. Cancellation will stop future subscription renewals, subject to the applicable subscription terms.

2. Refunds

Payments for PostMitra AI Pro are generally non-refundable once a subscription payment has been successfully processed, except where a refund is required under applicable law or approved by PostMitra AI on a case-by-case basis.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same subscription period due to a duplicate payment, please contact us with the relevant payment details. After verification, the duplicate amount may be refunded.

4. Failed Payments

If a payment fails, your Pro subscription may not be activated or may be suspended until a successful payment is received.

5. Accidental Purchases

If you believe a subscription was purchased accidentally, contact us as soon as possible. We will review the request and determine whether a refund is applicable.

6. Service Issues

If you experience a significant technical issue that prevents you from accessing a paid Pro feature, please contact us so that we can investigate and resolve the issue.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a payment issue, contact the PostMitra AI support team and provide your registered email address and relevant Razorpay payment details.

8. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or payment provider. The time taken for the refund to appear in your account may depend on the payment provider or bank.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about cancellations or refunds, please contact the PostMitra AI support team.
